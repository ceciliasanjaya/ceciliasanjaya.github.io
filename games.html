How to Play
  • 2+ players — one is the Psychic, everyone else guesses
  • A spectrum appears between two opposites (e.g. TOXIC ↔ NOT TOXIC)
  • Psychic: spin to see the hidden target, then describe a situation that lands there
  • Guessers: drag the dial to where you think the scenario falls
  • Use ← → keys to fine-tune; hold PSYCHIC PEEK to glimpse the zone
  • Hit REVEAL — score 4 pts bull's-eye down to 0 miss. Take turns!
