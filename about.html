engineering

Why I Build

Engineering, to me, isn't just about code or circuits — it's about understanding the foundations of the modern world and using that knowledge to create things that matter. I'm fascinated by how things work at every level, from silicon to software.

What I love most is the creative side: taking an idea from imagination to reality. Whether it's fabricating a transistor on a silicon wafer, designing a RISC-V processor, or deploying a full-stack AI platform — the thread that connects everything is the process of figuring it out.

I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of hardware and AI — where the physical constraints of silicon meet the limitless possibilities of intelligent systems. It's a space that feels like the frontier of everything interesting.

outside of work

Life Outside Engineering

I want to live a life full of experiences. Engineering is a huge part of who I am, but it's not all of me. I love being active and outdoors — hiking, trying new sports, exploring new places. There's something about physical challenge that resets the mind in a way that no screen can.

At the same time, I genuinely love quiet time: learning new skills for no reason other than curiosity, working on creative projects, or just reading something completely unrelated to engineering. I think the best engineers have wide-ranging interests.

Growing up between Indonesia and California also means I have a deep appreciation for food, culture, and travel. There's no better way to understand the world — or to think differently — than experiencing how other people live.
