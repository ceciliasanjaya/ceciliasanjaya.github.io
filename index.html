About Me

Hi! I'm Cecilia Sanjaya. I was born and raised in Indonesia, where I spent the first 12 years of my life before moving to Irvine, California. Growing up between two very different environments taught me to adapt quickly and stay curious — traits that have deeply shaped how I approach engineering and problem-solving today.

I've always been fascinated by how things work and how technology shapes the way we live. Engineering, to me, is not just about building devices or writing code — it's about understanding the foundations of the modern world and creating solutions that make life better. I especially love the creative side of engineering: taking ideas from imagination to reality, combining innovation and technology to design systems that solve real-world problems.

My long-term goal is to contribute to technologies that advance the future of computing — from the silicon level to intelligent systems — and build tools that have a meaningful impact on how people interact with technology. Whether it's designing hardware, building secure systems, or exploring AI, I'm excited about pushing boundaries and continuously learning along the way.

Outside of engineering, I'm someone who loves doing and experiencing as much as I can. I enjoy being active, exploring the outdoors, and trying new things — but I also love quiet time indoors, learning new skills or working on creative projects. Above all, I want to live a life full of experiences, learning as many lessons as possible and growing from everything I do.
